# Brand identity manual

**INTERNAL DOCUMENT · FOR BRIAN STEFANOVICH'S USE**

Brian Stefanovich — Software Architecture and Consulting

**Clarity before complexity.**

This manual brings together the foundations of the brand, its voice, the visual system and the rules of use. It is an internal reference document: before writing, designing or presenting any material under my name, I check here that the decision is consistent with this system.

## 1. Brand foundation

**Purpose.** To help founders, business owners and engineering leaders make better architecture decisions before the cost of a wrong decision becomes structural.

**Core message.** Better software starts with better decisions.

**Brand promise.** Clarity before complexity.

**Positioning.** Senior software architecture, consulting, modernization, integration and fractional technical leadership — diagnosis first, explicit trade-offs, and every recommendation tied to its business consequence.

### Service families

- Software Architecture & Advisory
- Product & Business Consulting
- Modernization & Integration
- Fractional Software Architect

### Audience

Founders, business owners and engineering leaders in Chile and international remote clients.

## 2. Voice and messaging

I speak in the first person singular — "I diagnose", "my recommendation". This is a personal brand, not an agency that says "we". The tone is calm, senior and specific: I explain consequences instead of impressing with jargon.

### Favor

clarity, decision, architecture, system, constraint, risk, trade-off, evolution, roadmap, evidence, maintainability, outcome.

### Avoid

disruption, cutting-edge, world-class, 360°, digital transformation, scalable solution, synergy, magic, guaranteed, future-proof.

**Capitalization.** Sentence case in headlines and body text. WIDELY TRACKED UPPERCASE is reserved only for technical labels in JetBrains Mono — eyebrows, navigation links, footer attributes, phase numbers. Never for body text.

Natural, plain English, with minimal jargon; I explain "fractional" the first time it appears. No emojis.

## 3. Logo

The symbol is a linear interface glyph, always monochrome, accompanied by the "Brian Stefanovich" wordmark in Montserrat 700. Five versions, each in light and dark variants.

- SIMPLIFIED
- MINI
- VERTICAL
- STANDALONE

**Usage:** the symbol is always monochrome, with no gradients or effects. Keep a minimum clear space equal to the height of the symbol around the logo.

## 6. Spacing and grid

Base unit of 8px. 12-column grid with a 32px gutter. Page margins of 90px, section vertical padding of 45px.

**4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 (px)**

**Technical frame.** Each section is framed with a hairline: 1px Fog on top/right/left, 2px Fog on the bottom — a "technical drawing" motif repeated throughout the brand.

> Section frame example

**Corner radii.** 8px (small) · 14px (default card/button) · 18px (hero card) · 9999px (pill/circular). Cards use no shadow — only a 1px inner Fog hairline (2px on light/elevated cards).

## 7. Iconography

Simple line icons, consistent stroke, round terminals, restrained corner radii. No filled, cartoonish or 3D icons. They inherit color from context via `currentColor`.

- COLUMN
- LAYERS
- BLOCKS

## 8. Motion principles

Motion is calm and purposeful — never decorative or continuous.

- 200–450ms transitions, soft easing (300ms ease by default).
- No rotations or continuous loops.
- Buttons use a slight brightness increase on hover, with no color change.
- Motion communicates hierarchy and state — it never draws attention to itself.

## 9. Usage — dos and don'ts

### Do

- Use Blueprint for action and Copper only for human consequence, sparingly.
- Keep the logo symbol monochrome and with clear space.
- Write in the first person, with a calm and specific tone.
- Reserve uppercase + JetBrains Mono for technical labels.

### Don't

- Don't use aggressive gradients, pronounced shadows or 3D effects.
- Don't give Copper the same visual weight as Blueprint.
- Don't use jargon such as "disruption" or "digital transformation".
- Don't use emojis or images of floating code or synthetic "AI".
